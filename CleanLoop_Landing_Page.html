One connected system

Everything needed to manage dump areas

From location registration to verified action, CleanLoop gives teams a shared view of day-to-day waste management work.

Dump Area Registry

Maintain a digital profile for every location, including ward, GPS coordinates, category, assigned team and inspection history.

Map-based Monitoring

View registered locations on a map and filter by ward, condition, priority or last update to identify where attention is needed.

Waste Condition Updates

Record clean, partially filled, nearly full or overflowing conditions with timestamps, notes and photo evidence.

Citizen Complaints

Let citizens report local cleanliness issues, attach photographs and follow complaint progress through a reference number.

Tasks & Inspections

Assign work to field staff, set due dates, collect inspection evidence and ask supervisors to verify completion.

Reports & Insights

Review operational trends, complaint ageing, inspection coverage and ward-level performance using consistent measures.

A clear operating cycle

From issue to verified resolution

A consistent workflow helps every report reach the right team and makes the outcome visible.

STEP 01

Identify

A dump area is inspected or a citizen reports a cleanliness issue with a location and supporting details.

STEP 02

Assign

An authorized officer reviews the issue and assigns a task to the relevant supervisor or field team.

STEP 03

Resolve

Field staff complete the work and submit notes, time and photo evidence through the mobile app.

STEP 04

Verify

A supervisor checks the submitted evidence and confirms completion or returns the task for action.
