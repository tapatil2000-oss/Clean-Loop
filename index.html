Government Action System

From Complaint to Cleanup

A transparent workflow for waste management.

1

Complaint Submitted

Citizen uploads the photograph, description and location.

2

Officer Review

Municipal officer reviews the complaint and assigns a team.

3

Cleanup Activity

Field team visits the location and carries out cleanup.

4

Verification

Supervisor reviews after-cleanup evidence and closes the complaint.

Complaint Statuses

Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Verified
